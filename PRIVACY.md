# Privacy

Statute stores your profile (council, situations, interests, work, progress) in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone. We run no server that could receive it; the feed you download is a public file that is the same for everyone.

## Your postcode

Your council, ward and town are worked out on your device from the official ONS Postcode Directory (Office for National Statistics, Open Government Licence). Statute downloads the small file for the first half of your postcode (for example `pc/SL1.json`) from its own site and finds your full postcode inside it. Your full postcode is not sent anywhere. Like any website, the site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which handles the file requests; that request shows only the first half of a postcode, which covers thousands of homes.

We do not ask for religion, ethnicity, sexual orientation or any other protected characteristic, because no legal obligation in the app depends on them.

Storing the profile on your device is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, which is the exemption under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. If you clear your browser data, the profile is gone; we cannot restore it because we never had it.

If you install the app and back up your phone, that backup belongs to you and your phone's provider, not to us.

There are no analytics on the profile. If we ever add anything that leaves the device, this page will say so first and the feature will be off by default.
